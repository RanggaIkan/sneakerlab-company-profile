================================================================================
                      🔧 CUSTOMIZATION QUICK TIPS
================================================================================

CHANGE BRAND NAME:
  └─ Open components.js
  └─ Find "KICKS" or "SneakerLab"
  └─ Replace with your brand name

CHANGE COLORS:
  └─ Open style.css
  └─ Find :root { ... }
  └─ Update --color-* variables

UPDATE WHATSAPP NUMBER:
  └─ Open components.js
  └─ Find FABComponent
  └─ Replace 628XXXXXXXXXX with your number

ADD NAVIGATION LINK:
  └─ Open components.js
  └─ Find nav-list section
  └─ Add: <li><a href="#" class="nav-link">Your Link</a></li>

CHANGE FONT:
  └─ Open index.html
  └─ Update Google Fonts link
  └─ Update style.css --font-family variable
